Six-month forecast

Inputs we need from you

Six things to agree. Everything else the model already knows.

01  /  The workbook

Four groups of tabs

The first sheet is Contents. Every tab links back to it.

You touch

Groups 2 and 3. Nothing else.

02  /  Providers

Who we buy from

COGS is paid on the provider’s terms, so the model needs the split.

We need

The real providers, their terms, and the split per country. All of the above is a placeholder.

03  /  Paying providers

And how late we pay them

The term sets the due date. The delay sets when the money really leaves.

We need

The days late per provider and country, and any month that runs differently.

04  /  Factoring

Denmark sells invoices to the bank

A factored invoice ignores the customer’s terms and pays on the bank’s clock.

We need

Which countries and months. Then the rates: 50% sold, 80% advance, 0.1% fee — the fee is our guess.

05  /  Costs

When each cost line pays

Twelve lines, four rules, set per country. A dropdown on the Cost timing sheet.

We need

The rule for each line, per country. Plus any month a line ran late — rent three weeks late in March pushes it into April.

06  /  VAT

How long we hold the VAT

We collect it on sales, pay it on purchases, and hand the difference over later.

Read it as

Cash we have already collected, sitting with us before it goes to the government. Norway holds it longest.

07  /  VAT

Three things to confirm

The rest of the VAT rules are fixed by law. These three depend on your business.

We need

An answer to each. All three are one cell on our side.
